Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who uses MentyMind

MentyMind is a private tool with a single user: its owner. It does not collect data from any other person.

2. What data is processed

  • The TikTok access token of the owner's own account, created when the owner logs in with TikTok.
  • Basic profile information of that account (for example the display name), as provided by TikTok.
  • The video files the owner chooses to upload.

3. How data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos as drafts to their own TikTok inbox.

4. Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. MentyMind does not use cookies or tracking.

5. Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access makes the stored token useless; the local copy is then deleted.
