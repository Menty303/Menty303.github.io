MentyMind

MentyMind is a private tool used by one creator to manage their own TikTok videos. It takes finished videos from their computer and uploads them as drafts to their own TikTok inbox. The creator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides personally whether to post it.

MentyMind does not post publicly on its own, does not read other users' data and is not offered to other people.
